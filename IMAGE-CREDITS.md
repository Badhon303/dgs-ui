# Image sources and permissions

All published photography and product illustrations are derived from the files supplied in `assets/`. No external stock photography, hotlinked media or generated building imagery is used.

- Original assets remain untouched. WebP derivatives are in `public/media/`.
- `src/data/asset-manifest.json` records source paths, original dimensions, responsive variants, blur placeholders, focal points, visual descriptions, SHA-256 hashes, perceptual similarity candidates and review notes for all 97 files.
- `src/data/asset-review.json` documents exclusions and editorial decisions. Six promotional/group images are withheld from the portfolio. The remaining 65 photographs are individually described; they are not asserted to be 65 distinct completed projects.
- Embedded business marks remain in photographs. No architectural geometry, material or project details have been generated or changed.
- The supplied `assets/logo.png` visibly identifies **Double Glazing Solutions**. The compact typographic DGS treatment and architectural application icon are original interface artwork.
- Client logos and team portraits are withheld until client relationships, identities and permission are confirmed.
- Manrope and Cormorant Garamond fonts are locally bundled from Fontsource; their SIL Open Font License texts are in `src/fonts/`. No remote font requests are made.

The supplier/business owner must confirm rights to publish the supplied photographs, illustrations, logo and any future client/team imagery. Folder placement is not evidence of a client endorsement or a copyright license.
